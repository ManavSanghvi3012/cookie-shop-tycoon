# Manav's Cookie Shop Tycoon

Double-click **index.html** to play in any modern browser. No install, server, or internet connection is required.

Start with $100 and run a cookie shop for 30 days. Choose your batch size, cookie price, and advertising budget, then open the shop to see your results. Baking costs $0.50 per cookie; baking and advertising must fit your available cash. Unsold cookies are donated at closing. Prices can range from $0.25 to $20.00. Advertising increases demand with diminishing returns, while higher prices reduce it. Daily foot traffic varies. The season ends after day 30 or when you cannot afford a single cookie.

Progress saves automatically in this browser when local storage is available. Browser settings may prevent saving for local files. Use Start over to begin a new season.

Run the game logic checks with `node test-game.js` (Node.js is only needed for development checks).
